# Demo video checklist — 3–5 minutes

Planned recording only; no video is claimed recorded. Use an authorized demo project, two separately signed-in sessions and approved disposable data. Keep passwords, cookies, tokens and private project administration off-screen.

- **0:00–0:30 — Original IMDb:** introduce its mobile discovery → details → Watchlist workflow. State that IMDb is the reference and TMDB provides this app’s data/ratings.
- **0:30–1:45 — Recreated discovery/details:** show mobile Browse, one genre, Popular/Top rated/Newest, title Search separated from Browse, Reset, Load more, reload and Back from details. Point out the labeled TMDB rating and useful fallbacks where available.
- **1:45–2:30 — Watchlist:** sign in privately as `alex`, save a movie, reload to show persistence, show duplicate protection and remove an approved disposable save. Keep at least two approved movies for the session.
- **2:30–4:00 — Cinema for Two:** select 2–5 saved movies, invite `partner`, copy the session link; open it in the other session, demonstrate sign-in/return if recording safely. Show independent selections, explicit submission, one-vote waiting and Refresh, then both-participant matching results and reload.
- **4:00–4:45 — No match:** use a second approved session with disjoint selections (or an intentional empty vote), then show the explicit no-match result. Explain the fixed shortlist and private ballots briefly.

Before recording: finish hosted smoke checks in [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md), verify both username mappings, prepare match/no-match examples, hide sign-in entry, and confirm 360/390px layout. After recording: author reviews playback and supplies the actual video link/file in the final submission.
